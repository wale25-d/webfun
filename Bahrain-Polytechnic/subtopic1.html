THE INSTITUTION 01 / 03

Education with a practical point of view.

Bahrain Polytechnic is a higher-education institution in the Kingdom of Bahrain. This student-built informational website is designed to help prospective students begin exploring the institution and locate useful next steps.

The website is an independent coursework project, not an official university service. Refer to Bahrain Polytechnic’s official channels for current admissions, programme, and service details.
